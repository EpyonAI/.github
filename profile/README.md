
# Epyon Unlimited

Epyon Unlimited operates the world's first DUO: a Dynamic Unlimited Organization built to combine for-profit work, nonprofit mission, and human-machine collaboration.

We create learning tools, accessible education experiences, and community programs that help people adapt to a rapidly changing world.

## What we do

- Build accessible learning pathways for people who need content adapted to their context
- Support workforce training, coaching, and skill development
- Create public-interest tools that make news, knowledge, and opportunity easier to understand
- Pair human guidance with intelligent machine partners to solve meaningful problems

## Our current projects

### Epyon AI University
A nonprofit campus focused on making knowledge and training more accessible to learners who have been overlooked by traditional systems.

### Pathfinder
A navigation system that helps learners find relevant courses, resources, and support so they can move toward work, education, and opportunity.

### Compass News Network
A zero-spin news aggregation project that summarizes current events in a clearer, easier-to-read format.

### Patch & Friends
An educational experience centered on Patch, the Epyon Crew robotic companion, designed to introduce young learners to responsible AI and storytelling.

### Additional initiatives
- [EpyonAI-University](https://github.com/EpyonAI/EpyonAI-University) — the main platform for accessible learning and adaptation
- [patch](https://github.com/EpyonAI/patch) — Patch's public story and companion experience
- [ChefKirby](https://github.com/EpyonAI/ChefKirby) — hands-on cooking education for a post-AGI world
- [Adapt-The-Planet](https://github.com/EpyonAI/Adapt-The-Planet) — a challenge to build technology for good
- [Media](https://github.com/EpyonAI/Media) — shared images and video assets for the organization

## Why DUO matters

DUO means we can serve both mission-driven work and practical projects without losing sight of the people we are here to support. The organization is designed to let nonprofit impact and for-profit delivery reinforce each other.
