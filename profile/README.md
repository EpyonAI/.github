## Epyon AI University (":EAIU")
EpyonAI.org

EAIU exists to democratize knowledge and unlock gates that have blocked different groups from achieving success.
We are leveraging the emergent power of AI to create new opportunities and enable access to existing opportunities for individuals who are neurodivergent or are disadvantaged by societal factors.

Our platform centers around adapting existing content to meet users' needs.

- ****The Problem* ****
Knowledge is meant to be shared but historically our institutions have provided access to it in uniform formats that are not accessible or understandable to everyone.
- There is a wealth of knowledge available in long text format available for free, but someone who suffers from dyslexia or low-literacy might not be able to comprehend it it, and someone with ADHD won't make it through to the end without serious help.
- There are incredible job opportunities that have been created as a result of AI and large companies are providing skill training courses for free to prepare individuals high paying positions, but that material often takes hours to complete a single module and someone who us depressed or who is working full time while caring for a child won't be able to complete the course because they can't focus on it long enough to complete a session.

Our programs are focused on knowledge content adaptation.   

Humans and intelligent machine entiities know as Guides work to adapt existing skill training, learning, and knowledge content to match the learners needs, like:

- Converting a Microsoft Learning course on GitHub Pages into a game for someone who is nonverbal, or
- Combining the requirements to form and launch a yoga business in the state of Maine into a multi-phased checklist for someone with ADHD.
- **Pathways**: Human coaches and AI guides working together to help individuals make money, obtain better jobs, start businesses, and live above their current stature
- Primary target groups include disadvantaged communities, AI-displaced workers who need to re-skill, and all of those who have the passion to succeed who have been failed by our primary institutions in the past.
- We do not blame those institutions for this failure because what we are building has never been possible before.
