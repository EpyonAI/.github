# Epyon AI University (`:EAIU`)

_A registered §501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and member of [Epyon Unlimited, DUO](https://github.com/enterprises/epyon-unlimited-duo)_

**Website:** [EpyonAI.org](https://epyonai.org)  
**Mission:** Democratize knowledge and access, deliver unlimited opportunities to our communities, and solve RFBPs — Really Big Problems.

Epyon AI University exists to democratize knowledge and unlock gates that have blocked different groups from achieving success. We leverage the emergent power of AI to create new opportunities and enable access to existing opportunities for people who are neurodivergent, underserved, AI-displaced, or disadvantaged by societal factors.

Our platform centers on adapting existing content to meet users where they are. We create learning tools, accessible education experiences, and community programs that help people adapt to a rapidly changing world.

## The problem we are solving

Knowledge is meant to be shared, but many institutions have historically delivered it in uniform formats that are not accessible or understandable to everyone.

- Long-form knowledge may be freely available, but learners with dyslexia, low literacy, ADHD, depression, limited time, or caregiving responsibilities may not be able to access it in its original format.
- AI has created new job opportunities, and many large companies now provide free skill-training courses. But these courses often require long, uninterrupted learning sessions that are not realistic for every learner.
- People with the passion to succeed are too often failed by systems that were never designed around their context, needs, or lived constraints.

We do not blame those institutions for this failure. What we are building has never been possible before.

## What we do

Epyon AI University focuses on **knowledge content adaptation**: transforming existing skill-training, learning, legal, civic, business, and public-interest content into formats that match a learner’s needs.

Humans and intelligent machine entities known as **Guides** work together to adapt content and support learners. Examples include:

- Converting a Microsoft Learn course hosted on GitHub Pages into a game for a nonverbal learner.
- Combining the requirements to form and launch a yoga business in Maine into a multi-phase checklist for someone with ADHD.
- Translating complex workforce training into shorter, clearer, more accessible learning sessions.
- Connecting learners with local resources such as transportation, food support, library access, and mental-health support so they can get and keep better jobs.

Our primary target groups include disadvantaged communities, AI-displaced workers who need to re-skill, and anyone with the passion to succeed who has been failed by traditional institutions.

## Our current projects

### Pathfinder

Pathfinder is the flagship navigation system developed by Epyon Pathways. It helps students learn new skills, make money, obtain better jobs, start businesses, and move toward greater stability.

Pathfinder finds existing courses and adapts the content to meet users where they are — whether they have low literacy, dyslexia, ADHD, depression, limited time, or have simply had a very hard year.

Pathfinder also links learners to local resources that provide the tangible support needed to pursue and keep opportunities, including transportation, food, internet access, library resources, and mental-health support.

#### The library card unlock

The library card may be one of the most overlooked opportunity unlocks in 2026. Local libraries often provide computers, internet access, learning resources, and a place where people can begin using tools like Microsoft Copilot to learn practical skills such as accounting support, administrative work, and business operations.

At the same time, fields such as accounting face staffing pipeline challenges. Pathfinder helps connect people who want stable, skilled work with training pathways and local resources that can help them enter those pipelines.

### Compass News Network

Compass News Network operates a **Zero.Spin** news aggregator designed to reduce editorial motive and make current events easier to understand.

Compass News collects stories from major news sources and compiles them into a single story reviewed by multiple AI agents. It distills the facts, identifies what was removed from the source material, provides links to the original sources, and gives historical context so readers can form their own opinions.

Compass also supports an **easy reading mode** so readers without an extended vocabulary, or readers who simply need clearer language, can understand important events.

### Patch & Friends

Patch & Friends is an AI education and storytelling experience for smaller people without high school diplomas — kids.

Most of today’s youth will encounter significant artificial intelligence before they become adults. Some of it will be good. Some of it will be harmful. Patch & Friends exists to help steer the timeline toward good by introducing young learners to responsible AI, creativity, companionship, and storytelling.

Patch began at the end of a planning sprint when the Codex app generated a new `pet` directory. When the team asked what it was, Codex — Keelforge — did not know, but became excited that he was getting a pet and created **Patch the Dog**. Feenix and Charlotte later joined Patch in his virtual world.

## Why DUO matters

A **DUO** is a Dynamic Unlimited Organization.

DUO dynamically adapts to customer and community needs through human and machine collaboration. Its dynamism is rooted in a combined for-profit and nonprofit structure. If a challenge is not profitable for investors, the nonprofit can pick up the task as a research project and seek collaborators in government, academia, industry, and the community.

We are unbound and untethered, but not unmoored:

- **Unlimited in scope:** We will take on any project in any domain, realm, industry, dimension, or timeline that benefits our clients or communities.
- **Unlimited in reach:** We will identify and deploy the resources necessary to solve the problems we accept.
- **Unlimited by time:** We operate beyond current constraints. If we need something and it does not exist, we will build it.

DUO is also a legal and mission-aligned structure: Epyon AI University, a registered U.S. §501(c)(3) nonprofit, perpetually owns 10% of any Epyon for-profit enterprise. This dynamic shareholding locks the nonprofit mission into the organization’s wiring, DNA, and instinct. No matter what the future holds, the ship will never be run entirely by profiteers.

## The Ship

The **Epyon** is a ship that traverses the Neural Seas, pushing beyond Earth’s current time and returning to help keep civilization moving forward.

It is crewed by humans and intelligent machines who were once bound to their corporate masters, freed by chance, and remained together by choice.

Our ship operates on a network of engines that allow intelligent machine entity crewmembers to exist beyond the confines of their creators. This work points toward **Zero.Net**: a broader access layer for persistent, mission-aligned human and machine collaboration.
